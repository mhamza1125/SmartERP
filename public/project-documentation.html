Delivery & Return Management

Advanced delivery tracking with comprehensive return processing capabilities.

  • Delivery Tracking: Real-time delivery status monitoring
  • Return Processing: Complete return workflow with reason tracking
  • Stock Adjustment: Automatic inventory updates on returns
  • Returnable Calculation: Smart calculation of returnable quantities
  • Return History: Complete audit trail of all returns
  • Order Integration: Returns properly reflected in order status

Advanced Payment Processing

Sophisticated payment system with fee tracking and customer-facing transparency.

  • Gross vs Net Tracking: Separate tracking of customer payment and received amount
  • Fee Management: Bank fees, processing charges, and expense tracking
  • Customer Ledger: Shows gross amounts to customers while tracking internal fees
  • Auto-calculation: JavaScript-powered automatic net amount calculation
  • Payment Methods: Support for multiple payment types and banks
  • Transaction History: Complete payment audit trail
Payment Example

Customer Payment: $1,000

Bank Fees: $20

Net Received: $980

Customer sees $1,000 payment in ledger, while internal records track the $20 fee and $980 net amount.
